Three shapes, three jobs, and a kingdom-specific name for each. Any buddy can field all three.

Named locally
Each kingdom renames its pals: Ember Pip and Lava Chonk at the Forge, Bubble Pip and Tide Chonk at the Lake, and so on. Same job, different hat.
Elemental body
Fire is a flame, Water a droplet, Earth a mossy rock, Air a cloud, Light a star, Dark a crescent moon. Role changes the silhouette's size, never its element.
Spark
Fills over time. Pals cost 2–4, so an empty spark bar means waiting, not clicking harder.

The Compact at a glance

The two numbers everyone quotes, plus the rules that keep the Little Wars little.

RuleDetail
Gatehouse120 health. Must fall before a core can be touched, per lane or at the maze exit.
Core360 health. Pop it to win. Rebuilt overnight by the Pips.
Final BounceAt 2:45 every remaining gate cracks and both cores are exposed.
Strong / weak+30% against the element you beat, −22% against the one that beats you.
No jumpingPals walk their lane. Lane choice happens at the gate, not mid-field.
Rail markersIn Tower Clash, the gold N ring and cyan S ring mark the next tower on each rail. Pick NORTH or SOUTH; both rails meet at the Master.
The wheel
Fire › Earth › Air › Water › Light › Dark › Fire. Six seats, one circle, no exceptions.
Spawn gate
Your kingdom's front door: Lava Vent, Tidewell, Mud Burrow, Cloud Chute, Sun Gate or Shade Well.
Rival's hearth
The rival brings their own kingdom and landscape. The battlefield is always shared ground.
Spawn Events
Roughly every 30–38 seconds the battle pauses and offers two bonuses. Take one or skip it for spark.

Glossary

Words the kingdoms use without explaining, and what they actually mean.

Hearth
A kingdom's founding spark, and by extension the kingdom itself. “Six hearths” is the polite way to say “six countries.”
Spark
The resource that pays for pals and towers. Every kingdom produces it by being extremely itself.
Core
The big building behind the gates. Popping it is a concession, not a casualty, which is why everyone is so cheerful about it.
Gatehouse
The 120-health door in front of the core. Its whole job is to be a formality that takes a while.
Pop
To bring a core to zero. The sound is described in the Compact as “satisfying and slightly rude.”
Muddle
Any dispute nobody can remember the cause of. See also: the War of the Long Muddle, the reason the Compact exists.
Buddy
Your kingdom's mascot and emergency power. Beloved. Slightly overconfident. On a cooldown.
Little War
A four-minute disagreement with a scoreboard, conducted under the Compact and followed by a rematch.
Rail
One of Tower Clash's two forward routes. North is NW → Master → NE; south is SW → Master → SE. Both sides choose a rail, and the rails meet at the Master.
Master Tower
The centre hub. Take it and the rival keep opens; lose it and your own keep is exposed.
