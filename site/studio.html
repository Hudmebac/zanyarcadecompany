Where the name comes from

Three letters, one Zac

Z is for Zany. A is for Arcade. C is for Company. Said quickly, it is “Zac” — which is also the name of the person who makes the games. This was not an accident, and he is not sorry.

Zac makes games the way the first fire told stories: start with something small and bright, then see who turns up. There is no studio building, no publisher and no roadmap with quarters on it. There is a desk, a notebook, a working test suite and a rule about finishing things.

The mark is not a letter — it is the six kingdoms. Six coloured dots around a dark centre: fire, light, earth, water, air and dark, with the hearth they all came from in the middle. That is also where the hot chocolate is.

House rules

How we make things

Four rules, written on the inside cover of the notebook. They have not changed since the first game.

1

Finish it

A small finished game beats a large unfinished one, every time. The last ten percent is the game.

2

Playable in a minute

If the tutorial is longer than the match, the match is not ready. Learn by tapping something.

3

No gates

No installs, no accounts, no launcher, no store page. Open a link and you are already playing.

4

Leave them smiling

Bright colours, kind rules, quick rematches. Losing should feel like a good bit of the story.

From the desk

A note from Zac

I'm Zac. I make small games and I like them to feel like toys you'd find on a good kitchen table: bright, sturdy, and easy to pick up. Zany Arcade Company is just me for now, plus a lot of hot chocolate. Yes, the initials are my name. No, I have not stopped finding that funny.

Elemental Little Wars started as a joke about a fire that got bored. It turned into six kingdoms, four ways to fight, no jump button and a headless test suite that runs in seconds. That is exactly the sort of thing I want this studio to keep doing — one small, finished, cheerful idea at a time.

Down, Down, Down is the second spark: an endless fall through cloud ledges with a storm above you. It has its own stage on Netlify and a dedicated button below. Same rule as always: small, finished and better with one more go.

If you play it, I hope you pick a kingdom on vibes and then get oddly loyal to it. Tell me which one. I will almost certainly have a favourite that week.

— ZacFounder, Zany Arcade Company
